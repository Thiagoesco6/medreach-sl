Core features

Built for real conditions in Sierra Leone

Designed to work on 2G networks, feature phones, and shared devices across urban and rural areas.

Clinic locator

Find the nearest government and NGO health facilities with live distance information.

Medicine availability

Real-time stock status for essential medicines at local clinics and health posts.

CHW directory

Connect with verified community health workers serving your district.

Works offline

Core features function without internet — critical data is cached locally on your device.

SDG Alignment

A digital public good for a healthier Sierra Leone

MedReach SL directly supports three United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, with SDG 3 at its core.

SDG 3

Good Health & Well-Being — bridging the gap between patients and care providers in underserved areas.

SDG 10

Reduced Inequalities — ensuring rural and urban populations have equal access to health information.

SDG 17

Partnerships for the Goals — built with open data shared across government, NGOs, and communities.
